**Shiz:**

Here's what I notice: you gave me two article ideas that are the same article wearing different clothes — "here's what AI did to my work" — and then immediately asked what the blog is even for. The second question is why you can't pick. You're not stuck on the topic. You're stuck on what the writing is _for_ now.

My read on the actual question: infinite games don't have endpoints, they have stakes. So the real thing isn't "when do I finish" — it's "what's the scoreboard, and is it still the one I care about." Yours used to be legible. Write tutorials → rank → list grows → courses sell. That machine had a number on it every month. AI took the top of that funnel out back and shot it. Nobody's googling their way to you the way they did in 2018. So the scoreboard went blank, and a blank scoreboard feels like "what's the endpoint" at 1am.

Which means the question underneath is: what do you want to be true _because_ you kept writing? Not traffic. The thing traffic was a proxy for.

Because if it's "people find me and buy courses," the blog's a marketing channel and you should be ruthless about it — and honestly it may not be the best one anymore. If it's "I think better when I write it down and some people get to watch," that's a different blog with a different bar, and the AI articles are exactly right for it. If it's a body of work — the thing that exists in twenty years with your name on it — that's a third one, and it doesn't care about either article.

Which of those three actually pulls at you? Or is it a fourth I'm not seeing.
