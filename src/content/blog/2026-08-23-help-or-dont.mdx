---
title: "Help, Or Don't"
description: "We're raised to believe helping others is the right thing to do. It's equally valid to know we don't have to help anyone — and that nobody needs to help us."
tags: ['musings', 'life', 'reflections']
---

Many of us are raised with the expectation that we have to help others — it's the right thing to do.

But it's equally valid to know that we don't have to help anyone — and therefore nobody needs to help us.

This is sobering if you give it a moment.
