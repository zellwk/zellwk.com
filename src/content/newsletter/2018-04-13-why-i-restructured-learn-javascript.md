I restructured Learn JavaScript—I took everything I wrote (about 9 chapters, or 60+ lessons), and rewrote them in a different format.

This was a difficult decision for me because I wanted to complete and release Learn JavaScript by July 2018. If I restructured the course, I will not be able to complete Learn JavaScript by the deadline I set.

But I restructured anyway.

In this video, I share why I restructured Learn JavaScript, what the new structure is like, and what’s going to happen to Learn JavaScript’s enrollment in July 2018.
