## Updates for Understanding Async JS

Good news. I've built the Login App with Facebook, Twitter, Github, and Google. I'm ready to begin writing the lessons and teach you how to set these up.

Another huge update to share with you: I've done lots of work for Understanding Asynchronous JavaScript, but most of these work won't show up in the course.

Here's why.

I've recently dove deep into researching web security to ensure that recommendations I make in Understanding Async JS are secure — and if they're not, I'm going to explain why we're doing this and what to do in a real production app.

These web security are auxiliary content that are critical to understand, but they shouldn't be included in a AsyncJS focused course. What I'm doing is writing lots of articles that I can point you towards.

One example is "Understanding CSRF Attacks", which is a huge 2,400 word that explains everything you need to know about CSRF Attacks, including how to set one up and how to defend against it. I was planning to send the article out this week, but something urgent came up so (yeah, [the server stuff](https://ckarchive.com/b/68ueh8hq67lg)). So I'm pushing it to next week.

Other examples include:

- Understanding Cookies
- Understanding sameSite Cookies
- Understanding how Fetch handles cookies

All these cookie stuff are here because cookies are actually one major way people save authentication information in webapps. You kinda need to know how they work (which is included to a small degree). But the full explanation resides elsewhere for your reading pleasure.

These articles are not ready yet. Some are not even written! Even the two articles I sent last week — [Serving HTTPS locally with Node](https://zellwk.com/blog/serving-https-locally-with-node/) and [Using node\_modules in the frontend without bundlers](https://zellwk.com/blog/node-modules-in-frontend-without-bundlers/) — are content related to Understanding Async JS (even though they don't look like it).

To sum it up, I'm working hard on Understanding Async JavaScript even though it doesn't look like I am! 😅.
