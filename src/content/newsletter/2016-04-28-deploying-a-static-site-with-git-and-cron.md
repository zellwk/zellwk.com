I love static site generators. They make it easy for me to experiment and tweak my sites without having to go through the Wordpress way of doing things. To top it off, I simplified my blogging workflow since I don’t need access to the Wordpress backend anymore.

The only major problem I had with static site is that that I’m unable to schedule my articles and publish them on a different date. I tried several methods, burned myself (I lost one article I’ve written).
