---
title: Preventing a Windows PC from adjusting the screen's brightness automatically
---

I noticed my Windows PC (Surface Laptop 3) changes brightness automatically in a dark room. This made my screen brighter and hurt my eyes (and it's so frustrating!).

I tried to prevent this PC from switching brightness automatically, but realized it's not as simple as it seems!

Turns out, Intel-based Windows Computer has this thing called adaptive contrast that changes the bright on screen based on how bright your screen is. (Sounds recursive? Yeah. I think this is stupid. I don't know why they built this in).

I wanted to share how I prevented Windows from adjusting the brightness of the screen.

**There are two steps:**

1. Turn off the "change brightness automatically" option
2. Turn off "adaptive contrast" from the Windows Registry

[Here's the article again](https://zellwk.com/blog/windows-brightness). Enjoy!
