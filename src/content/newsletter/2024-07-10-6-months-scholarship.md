## Sneak Peek of Splendid Labz

Splendid Labz is a library of lots of stuff:

- Astro componoents and scripts
- Svelte components (made and built to use with Astro’s Island Architecture)
- Layouts library that makes layouts much easier than writing normal CSS
- Styles with _sensible_ design tokens that helps you make your site pretty easily
- And more…
