Happy New Year!

How have you been in this new year?

As you already know, what you plan for at the start of the year determines what’s going to happen for the entire year, so it’s important for you to sit down, reflect, and plan ahead.

During this important time, it’s wise to take a look at your actions and see if they’re helping you reach your goals. If your actions didn’t help you reach your goals last year, they may not be able to help you reach your goals this year too. So the smart thing is to figure out why and make some changes.

Now that’s hard because there’s a lot of information on the internet that tells you what to learn… without considering whether that advice is actually great for you.
