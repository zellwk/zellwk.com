## Clarifying the relationship between Popovers and Dialogs

In my process of creating a `Popover` component for Splendid Labz, I stumbled upon a question:

What `role` should I give the popover to make it more accessible?

That question sent me tumbling down the ARIA rabbit hole and led me to a slew of articles about Popvers vs Dialogs that were, in my honest opinion, pretty confusing!

At the end of my research, I should couldn’t answer the question. So I stopped and pondered about the relationship between popovers, dialogs, and a couple of aria-related stuff.

This is an article that puts all of these things into [a clear mental model](https://css-tricks.com/clarifying-the-relationship-between-popovers-and-dialogs/) and makes them easier to understand and relate to each other.

(Hint: `Dialog` is a type of `Popover`).
