Last week I walked you through the very basics of Susy 2 in a tutorial.

This week, we're going to dive deeper into customising grids with Susy 2. Specifically how you can use Susy 2 to create a responsive website on the fly super easily.

If you have did responsive websites before, you know that calculating widths and writing media queries can be somewhat demanding after a while. Susy makes them simple, with the help of an additional gem.

In this tutorial, I'll show you how to change your layout with Susy at different media queries using this gem. (If you don't like to add a gem, I'll show you how to do without it too).
