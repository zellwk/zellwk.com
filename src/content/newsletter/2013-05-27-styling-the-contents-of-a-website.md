title: Styling the contents of a website
---

If this is your first attempt at trying to create a website, I imagine that you might be frustrated and overwhelmed with the whole process.

One problem that happened to me early on while I tried designing my own website was that I couldn't get fonts to turn bold or italic when I made it that way in the wordpress backend. It really got me jumpy and my blog posts were turning out wrong!

Little did I know, I forgot to include the styles for &lt;strong> and &lt;em> tags.

You might also have encountered other problems, or missed them out entirely. If you do, don't worry about it. Its just a process of getting to learn about web design.

To help you with this process, I compiled a list of 6 items which I think are extremely important to a blog content page.
