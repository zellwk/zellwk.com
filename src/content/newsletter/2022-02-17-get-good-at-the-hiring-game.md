## Get good at hiring

I received an email from Rigo who talked about his frustrations regarding getting hired.

I can relate, mostly because many people I talked with has this problem.

Unfortunately I don’t have really good advice on the hiring topic since I don’t play that game well. I’m not interested in getting hired.

But I do have one thing to say — whatever game you’re playing, get good at that game.

If you’re seeking employment, you have to [get good at the hiring game](https://zellwk.com/blog/get-good-at-the-hiring-game/).

In my case, I have to become good at the sales game (since I make courses and sell them for a living). It’s something I’m uncomfortable with but I have to work through it. Wish me luck!
