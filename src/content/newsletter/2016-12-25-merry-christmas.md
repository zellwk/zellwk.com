Merry Christmas!

I hope the year has been great for you so far! I’m on a road trip around the states with my lovely wife so I haven’t been diligent with emails for the past two weeks (and won’t be for another week or so). If you sent me any questions, hang on to them, I’ll get back to you soon 😊!

Anyway, since it’s Christmas, I want to share something useful with you! As you might know, I’ve been blown away by the awesome conferences over at Fronteers when I spoke there in October. The videos are finally up! Here are some I highly recommend you to check out:

- [Nadieh Bremer on Hacking the visual norm](https://vimeo.com/194817475) - This will blow your mind.
- [Jason Grisby on Adapting to input](https://vimeo.com/194967832) - This forces you to think about various input methods. We’re not just dealing with mouse, keyboard and touchscreen anymore.
- [Scott Helmes on Modern Web Security Standards](https://vimeo.com/194961863) \- This will clear up your doubts about intensely complicated security jargons like STS, PKP etc.
- [Sarah Drasner on Functional Animation](https://vimeo.com/194963386) - Animation is wonderful, so learn more about them!
- [Heydon Pickering](https://vimeo.com/194964404) - Definitely watch this and laugh your ass off while learning from Heydon. It’s so brilliant I don’t even know how to begin talking about it
- [Finally… yours truly on building responsive components with CSS](https://vimeo.com/194962824) - Here, I show you my thoughts on building responsive components.

Hopefully, that’s more than enough to take your weekend away 😜. (No, seriously, spend the holiday with your loved ones. Work and learning can wait till holidays are over).

With that, Merry Christmas and happy holidays! Party hard! 🎉🎉🎉
