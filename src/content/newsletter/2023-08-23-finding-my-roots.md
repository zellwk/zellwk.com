There’s something I have to tell you about.

It’s nothing related to you — but it’s something I have to get off my chest.

You see, I’ve pretended to be happy, okay, upbeat, and positive in my communications with you in the last couple of months.

But I haven’t been happy, okay, upbeat, or positive for a while now. In fact, I’ve done things I’m ashamed about too.

The discomfort towards my behaviours has been taking a hold of my emotional space. And it’s creating a huge depression.

This is a blog post where I share a little bit more about the truths that are going on right now. It’s not pretty. Maybe it isn’t even meant for you to read.

But in the light of pursuing truth and authenticity, [here it is](https://zellwk.com/blog/finding-my-roots).
