## A convo with Stefan Judis

This is one of the biggest things in my life right now.

I wanted to have conversations with interesting people for a long time, but I never worked out the courage to do so. I also never found an incentive for them to jump into a conversation with me. But I decided to give it a try anyway.

[Stefan Judis](https://twitter.com/stefanjudis) is my first guest in this new show. (Thanks Stefan!). We literally talk about anything and everything we're interested in. I thoroughly enjoyed my time with Stefan and I thought might find it enjoyable too.

We covered pretty interesting things! Here are some of them:

- Why there's no need to use 3rd party CDNs like unpkg and jsDelivr anymore
- Stefan's career journey and how he found himself in Devrel
- How Stefan curates and creates content for [Web Weekly](https://www.stefanjudis.com/newsletter/) newsletter

And more!

## Shoutout for Typogram

I also spoke to Wenting (who made [codingfont.com](http://codingfont.com) and I'll release a video on it soon. (This one is pretty interesting. We talked about typography and how one begins a journey from leaving their job to starting a business).

Wenting is launching a new product — [Typogram](https://www.typogram.co/) — which is a piece of software designed to help you make great logos. I am a person who values great logos but I could never design them.

Typogram gives us the tools and lessons we need to make a logo that looks great and conveys the message you want to convey. I'm quite excited about integrating Typogram into my design process!

[Have a look](https://www.typogram.co/) and see if you're interested!

## A new blog

My biggest new project is a [new blog](https://zellwk.life/).

Learning about life has been a pet peeve for me for the longest time ever. Besides writing about code, I spent hours (and tens of thousands of dollars) working through personal development courses to pursue the meaning of life, how to understand this thing we call life, and more importantly, how to live a great life.

But **figuring out life is one hell of a tough process**. I've been working on it for many years now. Once in a while, I manage to get some sort of illumination that leads to insightful articles like "[Trying your best is really bad advice](https://zellwk.com/blog/trying-your-best/)".

**I want to create a space for me to figure out life in more consistent manner.** That's the reason for this blog.

You can think of it like Marcus Aurelius's [Meditations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meditations) — where he wrote for himself. I'm writing for myself to pursue growth and evolution.

I'm writing this new blog publicly because I felt some of these writings are insightful and may help you in some way. Examples include:

- [On "wanting to be loved for who you are"](https://zellwk.life/blog/wanting-to-be-loved-for-who-you-are/)
- [Misunderstanding "Cherishing"](https://zellwk.life/blog/misunderstanding-cherishing/)
- [Higher is not always better](https://zellwk.life/blog/higher-is-not-always-better/)

Sometimes you can get to know the darker sides too, like

- My determination to [rebuild trust](https://zellwk.life/blog/rebuilding-trust/) in myself
- and how I'm [raging against myself](https://zellwk.life/blog/raging-against-myself/)

**I made this for people who are sick of the general "life" advice out there** — where everyone seems to be touting the same things. If those things really worked, we would have applied them somehow and made them work. But things didn't work. So we dig deeper.

This showcases my process of digging deeper, one article at a time.

- I share my experience.
- I share my struggles.
- I share my learnings.
- All of them are true and raw.

I think it's something rare.

I think it's something you'd appreciate.

So I want to share this with you.

[You can read the blog here](https://zellwk.life/blog)

Note: I will only talk about this blog occasionally in this newsletter since there's a huge topic difference between the two. If you want more of this type of content, please subscribe to the new blog!

Hope to hear from you.
