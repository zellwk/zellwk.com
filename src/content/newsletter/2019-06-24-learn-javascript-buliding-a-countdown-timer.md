How’s it going? Over the last two weeks, I designed and built a countdown timer. I also wrote down how to build one for yourself yourself.

![](https://files.convertkitcdn.com/assets/pictures/393/2246329/content_Screenshot_2019-06-24_at_3.07.44_PM.png)

The difference between this countdown timer and those you’ll find in tutorials outside:

1. Most countdown tutorials teach you how to count to **days**. But I show you how to count to **m****onths** and **y****ears**. (It’s not simple).
2. Daylight Saving Time and other time-related nonsense are all taken care of. I even have a lesson on Daylight Saving Time to share what you need to do about it.

You can find the lessons in the [Github Repository](https://github.com/zellwk/jsf/).

Have fun learning!
