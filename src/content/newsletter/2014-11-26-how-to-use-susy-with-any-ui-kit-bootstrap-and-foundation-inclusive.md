---
title: How to use Susy with any UI Kit (Bootstrap and Foundation inclusive)
---

One of the major problems I heard people have with Susy is that it is difficult to use Susy to quickly prototype websites. This is because Susy doesn't come with any UI kits that full featured frameworks like Bootstrap and Foundation have.

It's almost inevitable that you'll slow down with prototyping without any UI kits lying around, and that would be especially tough if you have a boss to answer to :(

The good news is, Susy can integrate with ANY framework you want to use. It can play nicely with major frameworks like Bootstrap and Foundation, or even your home-grown UI kit as long as you know how to integrate it.

I wrote a special article to help you integrate Susy with your kit of choice. I explained how to use Susy with the Bootstrap elements in this tutorial and it can be found at:

[http://www.sitepoint.com/use-susy-bootstrap/](http://www.sitepoint.com/use-susy-bootstrap/)
