Lemme continue on the teaching rift for one last article.

One thing I find irritating about coders is: we like to use big words (like our industry jargons). We feel smarter about ourselves when we use big words, but we leave people confused.

Many articles suck because of the big words we use.

If you want to write about programming, please tone down on big words. They’ll make articles much easier to understand.

In this article, I outlined three types of big words and how to simplify them. The three types of big words are:

1. Words that can be explained in a few words
2. Words that cannot be explained with simple words
3. Words that can mean different things in different contexts.
