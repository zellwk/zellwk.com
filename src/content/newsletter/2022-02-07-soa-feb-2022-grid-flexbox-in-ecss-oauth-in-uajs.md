## Life Beyond Code (LBC)

One thing I realized about LBC is that I'm beginning to write against concepts that seem widely accepted to be good. This is dangerous content because I'm going against the mainstream knowledge.

I'm glad I'm keeping LBC behind a paywall because my ideas are still in their infancy and I don't want to expose them to cynics. If you subscribed to LBC, thank you for allowing me to experiment with these dangerous ideas.

This month we talk about the idea of prioritization.

Most people think prioritization is a good thing and we should be doing it. The problem with prioritization is that people believe they know what's important to them. This is a fallacy.

Most of us don't really know what's important to us.

- Some things we think are important, are not important
- Some things we don't think are important, are actually important
- Some things we don't see their importance when we're alone, becomes important when we consider it with a group of people.

So what is important?

Prioritizing by "Importance" is a really bad way to about planning your life — because it assumes you have sorted out all the factors you deem to be important.

If this intrigues you then perhaps you may be interested to read the article. There's no web portal for LBC yet, so the article was delivered via email last Friday with the subject "The Prioritization Fallacy".
