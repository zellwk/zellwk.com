What is Life Beyond Code?

Life Beyond Code is a project I'm launching to **have conversations about life**, beyond the realm of code (as the title suggests).

This project came about because I noticed an increasing desire to talk about other aspects of life — which I feel are as important, if not more important than the realm of code.

Life is more than just work. Life is also more than spending our "free time" with our loved ones. It's also more than improving ourselves to take on larger challenges.

So what is life actually about?

How should we live?

I hope to examine my life so I can explore deeper into it. I want to live a life that's worth living. I want to have a conversation with you regarding the aspects of life I have explored, am exploring, and even things I've yet to explore.

Hopefully these conversations will inspire you to create a life that's fulfilling for you as well.

What’s in Life Beyond Code

**The ultimate aim for Life Beyond Code is to create a community where we can have conscious conversations about all areas of our lives**.

This community serves to deepen our understanding about our lives so we can create a life that we want to live. We can connect with each other and forward our lives together. I want this to be a sanctuary where you can stop by to recharge, recover, and walk back into your life.

I want to begin building this community by starting where I can — by sharing my life with you.

So in this very first phase of Life Beyond Code (at a super alpha stage), I'd like to start with the following:

- I'll write one article about my explorations in life each month
- We'll have a video call every quarter to connect with each other. If you're up for it, we will also discuss how to grow this community together.

My explorations into life

I had lots of questions that couldn't be answered by society. Questions like:

- Why do I feel so different from other people around me?
- Why do I want something that's different from what everyone else wants?
- Why is it bad to want what I want?
- What do I actually want?
- What is important to me?
- What should I want to do with my life?
- How do I want to live my life?
- How do I choose where to go?
- What stops me?
- How can I overcome these obstacles?
- How do I make the right decisions?
- How can I be sure I made the correct decision?
- Why do I hesitate?
- Why do I stop myself from going after what I want?
- What am I born for?
- Why do I exist?
- Who am I?
- What does it mean to live a meaningful life?
- What is meaningful?

Society has never been able to give me satisfactory answers for these questions. It always found a way to squelch my questions, by making me wrong, by beating me up, by saying that I'm doing too much and I should tone down.

As a result, I haven't been able to express my desires, feelings, or even thoughts for many years. I would always feel a numbness in my chest whenever I had to talk to people. This continued on for many years and I thought it was normal.

But this isn't normal.

Society simply didn't do a good job of grooming me to become the person I can be. Society doesn't want to — because everyone wants you to be someone for them. They don't want you to be who you are. If you become who you are, you can't be the person they want you to be.

It's also a fact that most people never see you for who you are. They don't actually hear what you're trying to say. They don't understand you either. Most people simply see you as a projection of who they want you to be. And as a result, most people don't feel heard. (Myself included).

We're the same too — we see people for who we want them to be. We rarely (if ever) see others for who they truly are.

I never knew these facts until I went through a [life coaching course](https://zellwk.com/blog/tcc/) back in 2014. In this course, I realized everything I was doing up till this point in my life was to hear four words from my mom — "I'm proud of you".

During the course, I picked up the phone. Called my Mum and asked her. "Mum, are you proud of me?". She replied: "Of course I am. If I'm not proud of you, who will I be proud of? You're my son okay. Everything you do I will be proud of you".

This helped me put down a huge stone in my heart. I saw the relationship between my mum and I in a new light. I knew I don't have to do anything again in order for her to be proud of me.

My next step was to be proud of myself.

I wanted to prove to myself that I am worthy. I did this by [creating and selling my first book](https://zellwk.com/blog/how-i-wrote-and-sold-my-first-ebook-and-how-you-can-do-it-too/), [Learn Susy](https://learnsusy.zellwk.com/). This proving act eventually led to me creating other courses, blog content, and invariably led to the reason why you're reading this today.

Since 2014, I have taken multiple other courses to broaden my knowledge and understanding of various aspects of life. This includes working on my emotions, my intellect, my physical body; working on my relationships with people and groups; and learning esoteric stuff like energy and healing work.

Although I am not ultra successful in any domain, I feel I gathered sufficient knowledge and data to begin sharing with you what I noticed about life.

What will I be writing about in Life Beyond Code

Honestly, I don't know at this point.

What I know for sure is I'll be writing about my experiences in life. It may be something I feel deeply for. It may be something I'm inspired by. I may write about an experience I've been through and what I felt about it.

The only thing I can promise you is — these articles will be candid. I'll expose my emotions and thoughts, even the darker ones, that people generally don't dare to talk about. The agenda will always be to enrich your life by sharing the experiences I have.

Possible topics I have on hand are:

- How I walked out of my depression
- Thoughts and feelings about a conversation with a friend who's currently stuck in a job he dislikes
- How my experience with music changed after getting a great pair of speakers
- Sharing with you how I worked through some of my art related trauma and possibly showing you the art pieces I created over a 2-month program.

I will also give you the opportunity to ask questions you're interested about and I may riff off those questions instead.

One article per month. Something real. Something heartfelt. Something that will enrich you as a person. Maybe it will change your thoughts or beliefs.

Maybe it will help you walk out of a dark emotion. Maybe it will inspire you to take action towards a goal you'd like to achieve.

Either way, I don't know what I'm going to write about at this point. I'll know when I begin to write it near the end of this month. (Being super candid).
