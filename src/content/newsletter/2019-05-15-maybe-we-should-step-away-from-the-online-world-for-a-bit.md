---
title: Maybe we should step away from the online-world for a bit
---

I woke up at 3am and had to write this article.

Lately, I’ve been thinking about the content we consume in our echo chambers. For me, it’s Twitter.

And the conversations in my Twitter feed are driving me nuts. I see things like:

1. Arguments about whether CSS is a “programming” language
2. Bitching about how Accessibility and Performance sucks on certain sites
3. Why you SHOULD learn frameworks vs why you SHOULDN’T learn frameworks.

There’s so many opinions flying around. Most of these opinions are complains. People spread anger, fear, and worry in our communications. Daily

This should stop.

But I realize it won’t. Because we can’t control what people say or do. (We can mute them, but it doesn’t solve the problem).

What we can do is take a break and ignore what everyone else has to say. If we ignore opinions and explore beyond our comfort zone, maybe we can find what truly calls for us.
