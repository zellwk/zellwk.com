title: No video this week. Here’s a worthwhile lesson instead.
---

Were you hoping to get a video today? If you are, sorry to disappoint you. You don’t get a video today. I trashed the video I made because it wasn’t good enough. I wanted to remake it, but shit happened.

Real shit.

First, the government forced me to renovate my toilets. I had to wrap my house up so I don’t have to scrub the house for 3 days when the renovation completes. Here’s what it looks like now:

![](https://convertkit.s3.amazonaws.com/assets/pictures/393/1579104/content_house.jpg)

This meant I don’t have my recording equipment with me. I wanted to setup a mobile recording station, but I forgot to take my headphones and microphone with me when I wrapped up the house 🤦♂️.

But I wouldn’t have been able to record the video even if I had my recording equipment with me. Something real bad happened.

My grandfather died last Monday.

We held a funeral for him that ended on Friday. I didn’t have the space and energy to work on the video the entire week.

Why am I telling you all this?

I told you previously that [the show must go on even when life shits on you](https://zellwk.com/blog/what-to-do-when-life-shits-on-you/ "What to do when life shits on you"). While that’s true, sometimes life shits on you so hard, you’re forced to stop everything you intended to do.

And if it happens, it’s okay.

It’s part of life.

Don’t blame the situation. Take responsibility for everything that happens. You can work on something else with the time, space, and energy you have.

I spent a lot of energy on communicating with my grandpa before he died. And even after his death, I spent most of my energy around him. It was worth it.

Another example: I wouldn’t have been able to write this article today if any of the misfortunes that happened to me this week was missing. I was only able to share the lesson I’m sharing because they happened.

Instead of lamenting the situation, I took the lesson and shared it with you. I viewed the misfortunes as a blessing instead.

If you’re stuck in a lousy situation you can’t control. Take a moment to reframe what’s happening to you. Maybe it’s not as bad as you thought it was.

Maybe you can find a way that lets you move ahead powerfully.

I hope you find it.
