Happy 2016! :)

How was 2015 for you? I realized I haven’t taken stock of my life for the past 27 years, so I decided to do a year-end review for 2015.

It was the best decision I made in 2015. I became happier, and more grateful.

I realized I focused too much on the negativity of everyday life, even in the last week of the year. I hated how I didn’t manage to write more, code more, design more… so much that I completely forgot about the amazing things that happened.

I want to urge you to do a year-end review. It might help you just like how it helped me. Here’s my review if you’re interested:

[http://zell-weekeat.com/2015-review](http://zell-weekeat.com/2015-review)

Hope you have a great year ahead.
