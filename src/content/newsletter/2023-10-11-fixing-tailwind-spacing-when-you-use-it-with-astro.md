---
title: Fixing Tailwind Spacing when you use it with Astro
---

When using Tailwind with Astro, you’ll notice that [Tailwind’s space utilities](https://tailwindcss.com/docs/space) stop working when you use it with Astro Components or Slots.

In this article, I’m going to show you how to fix this with a simple plugin that I’ve built.

[Here’s the link to today’s article](https://zellwk.com/blog/tailwind-space-in-astro).

Enjoy!
