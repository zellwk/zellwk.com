Happy new year.

I didn't want to send you an email today. I was afraid to disturb to on 1st January, when you're happily enjoying your holidays; but something urgent and important came in and I felt compelled to write to you.

Just five minutes ago, I received an email that says this:

![](https://convertkit.s3.amazonaws.com/assets/pictures/393/982201/content_Screen_Shot_2018-01-01_at_4.15.56_PM.png)

If anyone of you are feeling the same as this lady who wrote back, I want to tell you this:

**You aren't too young, too old, too male or too female to learn to code. You can code. You can become a great developer. You can make things happen.** I know people who're female, and people who are over 70 who's learning and making a ruckus. **I am here to support you on your journey** **if you want to learn frontend development.** **Ask me questions so I can help.**

**Join me as a frontend developer in 2018.**

If you don't feel that you can never succeed at this coding thing, please help someone you know overcome their fears. If you can't, please share this message with them and send them over to me. I'll try to help.

Thanks.

Here's to a great 2018.
