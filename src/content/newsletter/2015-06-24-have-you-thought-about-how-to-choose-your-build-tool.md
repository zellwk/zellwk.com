title: Have you thought about how to choose your build tool?
---

Choosing to use one tool over another is probably one of the hardest choices developers like us have to make, regardless of whether we’re choosing a code editor, a framework or a build tool.

As you know, the two most popular build tools right now as you know, are Grunt and Gulp. It’s already difficult to choose between these two.

Things get worse if you’re already using another build tool. Should you ditch yours for a newer, shinier tool like Gulp?

It’s a difficult choice you may be battling deep inside. And even if you decide to choose Grunt or Gulp now, what happens if a better tool comes up in the future? Will you once again switch?

That’s why I wanna share with you my process of choosing tools, and how I went from using Codekit, to grunt, and eventually settled on Gulp.

You’ll hear the truth about my process and you’ll find out how I made my decisions. You’ll also hear about what I’ll do if a tool better than Gulp pops up.

All you have to do is to click on the link below, read through the post and let it simmer for a while.
