This something was inspired by Svelte — I got hooked by the way Svelte handles reactivity.

Just in case “reactivity” sounds like a huge terminology to you, it simply means: When something changes, something else should change (and be reflected in the DOM) at the same time.

Now this whole reactivity thing is much better explained with examples so reading [today’s article](https://zellwk.com/blog/reactivity-with-vanilla-javascript) would make much more sense immediately.
