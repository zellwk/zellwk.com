You know how hard it is to get a job as a frontend developer nowadays. Many companies are requesting for insane requirements. Many people have sent countless job applications only to hear nothing.

One guy event sent 650 job applications to get one job offer (and it's not even a frontend developer job...)

This is demoralizing for many beginners. I hate this.

Lucky for me, I didn't get into web development by looking at job portals. It's a sucky experience. I don't even think I'd want to apply via job portals even today.

There's a better way.

When I began web development, I hung out with people and stumbled into my first freelance web development job because they thought of me. It was simple as that. But I wasn't able to find a way to make this process repeatable for others.

Luckily, Devon Campbell managed to make his process repeatable. He's also taught other people how to get started with web development the same way — by finding a way to freelance instead of constantly apply for jobs via job boards.

You can find out more about Devon's course: [How to Start Earning Money as an Independent Web Developer](https://raddevon.com/how-to-start-earning-money-as-an-independent-web-developer/)

I know it may hurt my sales process since I'm launching Learn JavaScript in two weeks. But that doesn't matter. I want to let you know of a way to get into the profession, because I know this matters to you.

If you're struggling with getting your first job (or even your first freelance job) as a web developer, consider checking Devon's course out.

Hope this helps you.
