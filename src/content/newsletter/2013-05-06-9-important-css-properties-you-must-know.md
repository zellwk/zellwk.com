title: 9 important CSS properties you must know
---

It may seem extremely difficult to begin with CSS, especially when there are so many things to remember.

I know I felt that way when I first began learning. I took out my notepad and furiously wrote down every single CSS property I came across as I felt lost and afraid of not being able to successfully learn CSS.

It turned out that copying everything didn't work out. My brain wasn't great enough to contain everything, and I felt lost and hopeless.

You may have felt the same way.

When I coded my first website, I realized that you don't need to remember everything. You just need to have a firm grasp of the concepts, the few important points, values and what they do.

Which is why in this post, I've simplified what you need to know into 9 important points. Click here to see the blog post.

Take the knowledge you received from this post and code away!

Let me know if you encounter any difficulties anywhere.
