## Making sense of Starting Style

`@starting-style` is one of those much-awaited CSS features that lets you transition an element from `display: none` to something else, so I’ve been quite excited to use it!

But, as I sank my teeth into this piece of juicy meat, I realize there were bones (problems) everywhere — that it was difficult for my developer brain to grasp.

I finally figured out why and I wrote this article hoping to spread a better sense of this awesome feature.
