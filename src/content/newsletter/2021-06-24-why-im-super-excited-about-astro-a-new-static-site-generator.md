title: Why I'm super excited about Astro (a new static site generator)
---

I found out about [Astro](https://astro.build) (a new static site generator) while reading [CSS Trick's newsletter](https://css-tricks.com/newsletter/255-thoughts-on-astro/). I knew I had to check it out since both Chris and Robin were excited about it. And boy, I'm excited too, even though I only read the [Github Readme](https://github.com/snowpackjs/astro)!.

I want to share [why I'm excited about Astro](https://zellwk.com/blog/astro-thoughts) today.

In summary:

- Astro gives you the ability to use any framework
- No need to wrangle with complex workflows
- It does partial hydration
- It gives a MDX capabilities without having to write JSX
- It comes with scoped CSS by default without having to write them inside JS files!

I'm super excited to give Astro a whirl. I might even use Astro to rebuild this website since I'm working on a redesign anyway.

I hope you enjoy it as much as I do!

[Click here to read the article](https://zellwk.com/blog/astro-thoughts). Enjoy!
