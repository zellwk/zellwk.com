## I’m beyond excited to bring you the Accessibility module

For good reason! Because I’m covering WAY MORE than anything you’ve seen out there.

There’s really few good accessibility content out there. One of the rare ones is Heydon Pickering’s Inclusive Components.

Most accessibility tutorials out there tell you stuff like:

1. Accessibility is simple! (Just don’t fuck it up)
2. Accessibility is simple! (Just use right HTML elements and you’re good).
3. Accessibility is simple! (Just label your fucking form elements)
4. Accessibility is simple! (Just use the right ARIA attributes when you should)

(Okay, you can tell I’m pretty pissed with the content out there 😂).

Most tutorials oversimplify accessibility. They want you to have confidence that you can do it too. Much like how most people oversimplify JavaScript. (But we know it's not as simple as they say).

**Accessibility becomes a beast **when it comes to JavaScript**.** It’s a whole new world. There’s lots of ARIA roles, properties, and states you need to get familiar with. And I was paralysed with this huge topic for a long long time.

But I’ve worked it out. I’ve sorted things through. And I’m ready to teach you accessibility in the way you’ll receive it the best.

Step by step. Theory and Practical.

I have you covered.

I go so detailed that I even show you the inconsistencies between screen readers (through tiny video clips, much like GIFs) when I walk you through the components.

It’s gonna get exciting!
