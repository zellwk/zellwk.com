Happy Friday, Zell.

I want to share something unique with you today. **It’s about communication.** Everything breaks down if we don’t communicate with people—relationships with families, colleagues, or even internet friends like you and I are cannot survive without communication.

We cannot operate while alienating ourselves from everyone else, but many of us do that, myself included.

So, today, I want to share a video of a talk I gave at JSConf.Asia this year. It’s called “**what writing about frontend development taught me about communicating with the world**”.

In this talk, I bare it all—I shared what I learned, and even what hurt me in the past, so we can all learn to become better communicators.

I hope you enjoy it. Many people told me it’s incredibly motivating.
