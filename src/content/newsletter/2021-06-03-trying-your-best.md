I want to explain why it doesn't work and what pitfalls you might want to watch out for if you use the same strategy in your life.

This one is going to be super helpful for you if you fall into one of these situations:

- You subscribe to the trying your best mentality
- You're struggling with tutorial hell 🙂
- You feel like you've tried a lot, but you're not making any progress

Maybe it's time to stop trying. 🙃.
