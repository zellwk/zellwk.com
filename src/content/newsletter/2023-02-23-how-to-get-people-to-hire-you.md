If you’re like most people, you’ll wonder about three questions when people don’t hire you:

1. Are you good enough?
2. Did you not get chosen because others are much better than you?
3. How much better do you have to become before others will hire you?

These are good questions to think about, but most of the time, the real reason you didn’t get hired it’s not because of your technical capability.

The real reason is something else.

Today, you’re going to discover what this reason is — and once you know this, you’ll be able to change the way you position yourself in every area, and you’ll become the most attractive person to employers and clients.

Let’s go.

## We’re going to start with a shocking truth that nobody wants to hear

This is the reason why I got you to get out of your comfort zone — because once you hear and internalize this principle, you will never see jobs the same way again.

Here’s the principle you have to internalize.

**Nobody wants to hire anyone**.

So naturally, nobody wants to hire you too.

**They only hire because they need to.**

You know this is true — because if you can help it, you wouldn’t want to give away your money to other people either.

You only give it to others because you need to.

- You buy food, water, and other necessities only because you need them to survive.
- You buy a computer only because you need it to work.
- You buy a phone only because you need it to communicate with others

Companies are the same. You can even imagine companies as humans because they are operated by humans — so \*\*they will only hire you if they need you.

This is because money is the lifeline of a company — if a company runs out of cash, it’s over — so people who manage companies are as careful about money as you are.

**Once you see this, you will begin to understand why people get laid off too** — it’s because these people are no longer needed by the company.

This is a painful truth to see if you got laid off recently, but it’s a truth you need to see…

Because until you see this truth, you’re going to repeat your mistakes, get into non-critical positions, and potentially get fired again in the next downturn.

Now you know this truth, you’ll be able to put yourself in a position where you are needed — and therefore indispensable even in recessions.

## How to get people to hire you

To get people to hire you…

- You need to find out what the company needs
- You need to be able to fill that need.
- And you need to position yourself as the best candidate (if not the only candidate) to fill that position.

This sounds almost impossible because there are so many amazing developers out there — how will you ever become the best?

You only think this way because **you have been misled into thinking that coding ability is everything, but it’s not**.

And I’m going to prove it to you now.

Just imagine you’re going to buy a car right now and you’re choosing between a Ferrari and a Toyota, and your only criteria is that it can travel from one place to another place.

Now since both cars fulfill the need of traveling from one place to another, and the Toyota is cheaper, you will naturally choose the Toyota over the Ferrari.

Companies will also do the same — if a Junior developer is all they need, they won’t pay a Senior developer to do a Junior developer’s job.

This means **you don’t have to be the best developer in the world — you just have to be the best in the category of developers you’ve chosen to be in.**

This realization is going to be a relief for many people. But for some of you, you’ll still think it’s impossible because you’re not even the best in your category yet.

And here’s my answer.

You only think this way because **you have been misled into thinking in one dimension — coding ability — but coding ability isn’t everything**.

If it was, we wouldn’t have stories where rockstar developers get fired from their companies even though they were amazing at code.

What this means is **there are other factors — beyond technical ability — that influence the company’s need**.

And if you know what these factors are, you will be able to position yourself to meet these factors.

When you can position yourself to meet more of these factors while nobody else knows what the factors are… you’ll naturally stand out and become the best candidate for the job.

**This is how you actually stand out**.

This is how you get the job.
