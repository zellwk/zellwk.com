I've been asking myself "_what do I blog for_" subconsciously for over a year — without really allowing myself to sit down and answer that question, truthfully, honestly, and in a way that brings out the best of me.

Today, I sat down and [gave it an honest thought](https://zellwk.com/blog/the-infinite-game-of-blogging/) — with the help of my AI. (You'll get to see a transcript of our conversation).

This changes how I show up.

Maybe for the better.
