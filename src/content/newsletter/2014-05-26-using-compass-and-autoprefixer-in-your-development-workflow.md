A couple of months ago, I chanced upon Autoprefixer and have fell in love with its ability to automatically add vendor prefixes to all of my CSS codes. During that time, you either had to use the terminal or very code heavy stuff like grunt in order to use Autoprefixer.

Thankfully, Autoprefixer has came to Codekit 2 as well! I jumped at that sight and bought Codekit 2 immediately. To my dismay, Autoprefixer on Codekit2 doesn't work with Compass :(

I really loved Compass Vertical Rhythms, but I also wanted to power of Autoprefixer with the ease of use in Codekit. Hence, I came up with a quick hack to use all of them at once!

Check it out here: [http://www.zell-weekeat.com/codekit-2-autoprefixer-with-compass/](http://www.zell-weekeat.com/codekit-2-autoprefixer-with-compass/)

If you didn't know what Compass or Autoprefixer are, that article probably wouldn't help you very much.

Before you dismiss this email entirely, I wanted to let you know that both compass and autoprefixer are superb additions to every developer's workflow and you'll never go wrong understanding what they do.

I wanted to help you understand more about Compass and Autoprefixer as well, so I wrote up another article before sending this email to you :)
