Yesterday, I gave you a great mind hack to stand out from your peers: [Go beyond what your peers offer](https://ckarchive.com/b/o8ukhqhkk49ww).

This is a powerful concept.

But it puts you into a position where you need to be good. At the very least, you need to be better than your peers.

Here’s the problem.

**How can you be better than your peers?**

Remember your experience when you scrolled through a job board in the past?

When you scrolled through the job board, you noticed that the skills required for junior software developers exceed your capability. Even the most basic of jobs require something advanced like React or some JavaScript framework.

It’s hard as hell to learn these things, much less get a job.

So you got upset, demoralized, and dejected. But you’re a fighter and you picked yourself back up. You decided to learn the skills that are required so you can get hired.

I salute you. Because it’s hard to pick yourself back up.

And I hate to break the news to you, but trying to learn every skill listed in the job advertisement is a bad strategy. Why? Because everyone else is doing it. If everyone else is doing it, you blend in and you don’t stand out. Remember?

Besides, how many years do you want to dedicate your life to learning code before you finally get paid good money to do the work?

If you choose to work only on your technical skills, you’re just postponing the inevitable. You can be learning web dev for years. You can be coding in your bedroom for years and you still haven’t found a job yet.

When do you want to get a job? In 3 years or 3 months?

The truth is: You can’t get a job because you’ve been looking at the wrong things (just like everyone else, so don’t beat yourself up):

- You’re looking at the wrong places
- You’re looking at the wrong companies
- You’re looking at the wrong timeframe
- You’re looking at the wrong self

There are lots of things to unpack here but I will give you a hint.

The right timeframe is today.

**You have to decide that you’re ready for a job today**

It’s hard, but you have to take a leap of faith.

Once you decide you’re ready for a job today, everything else begins to fall into place. You’ll begin to look at the right places, you’ll begin to learn the right mindsets and skills, you’ll look for the right companies to work with, and you’ll look at the right self to portray outwards.

You’ll see how to manoeuvre yourself into a web dev job in 3-6 months easily. In fact, if you do this right you may even land a web job in the next 2 weeks.
