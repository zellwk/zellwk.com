---
title: Lessons learned from a project from hell
---

I worked freelance on a project a long time ago. Lemme call it project T.

Project T went really bad. I estimated it should take 3 months, but it took 9 months. Worse, even though I worked for 9 months, I didn’t get paid 9 months worth in money. I had to give a huge discount to the agency I worked with because they lost money on the project too. 😢

But well, that’s already over. I finally found the courage to write about the project as a case study, from a management point of view, on how this project failed.

I hope that you can learn something from this failure that I had to go through.

Here’s the article. Enjoy :)
