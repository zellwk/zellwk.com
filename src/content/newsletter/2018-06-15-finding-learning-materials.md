You can find almost anything you want in the first three pages of Google.If you cannot find it, it can mean four different possibilities:

1. The answer is already there, but you don't know it.
2. You don't have enough knowledge to understand the answer yet
3. You don't know what to search for
4. The answer might not be on google

In today's video, I explain what you should do in each situation. I also give you two tips to find good materials to learn from.
