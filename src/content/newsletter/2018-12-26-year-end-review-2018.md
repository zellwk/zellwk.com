It’s Decemeber 26 today. 2018 is going to end in a few days. Everything that has happened, whether good or bad, is past us now. It’s time we let go the pains and sorrows we accumulated in 2018. It’s also time for us to consolidate our lessons so we begin 2019 powerfully and intentionally.

**How did your 2018 go?**

If you haven’t done it already, take some time to write a year-end review for yourself. It’s easily one of the most powerful things I do every year.

When I create a year-end review, I take stock of what has happened for myself in the year. It helps me understand the changes I went through, and it helps me prepare a new mental state for the upcoming year.

Here’s my review this year: [https://zellwk.com/blog/review-2018](https://zellwk.com/blog/review-2018)

I suggest you do a review for yourself too. Feel free to answer any of these questions:

1. What went well?
2. What did you achieve?
3. What didn’t go so well?
4. What struggles did you face?
5. What lessons did you learn?

Then, once you’re done writing taking stock for 2018, decide what you want to manifest in 2019. Answer these questions:

1. What is 2019 going to be about?
2. What do you want to achieve?
3. How are you going to achieve it?
4. What’s going to stop you from achieving it?

This is just a simple format you can use to create a good your for youself in 2019. I use a slightly different format for my own review because I’ve been writing for a while. My writing is quite free-flow. You can take a look at [my review here](https://zellwk.com/blog/review-2018 "Year end review 2018") if you like.
