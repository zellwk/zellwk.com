Did you know that I’m working on a book called “Automating your workflow”? You’ve been missing out if you didn’t! Here, check this page out!

Anyway, I realized I haven’t talked about the book at all, and I might have held you in much suspense. So today, I’m gonna release some info :)

First of all, it was tough to come up with the contents of this book. I didn’t want to write a book just to teach you about Gulp. It wasn’t good enough.

What I wanted to achieve instead, was to guide you through the process to setup your workflow from scratch, while teaching you how to use Gulp to do that.

So this book is divided in to 8 major sections.

Section one walks you through the basics of automation, teaching you things you need to know to begin the journey, like giving you an overview of a development workflow, how to setup your project, how to use Gulp etc.

In sections two to seven, we dive deep into the six different parts of an development workflow and figure out how to automate each of them.

Finally in section eight, we go through how to iterate on your workflow, organize your Gulpfile and how to find out about good plugins to use.

You wanted to hear more about sections two to seven? Okay here it is:

Section two deals with the development phase. You’ll learn to reduce the amount of manual work you do, write code that’s easier to understand and speed up processes you use.

Section three focuses on testing. You’ll learn how to test, what to test for and get a peace of mind that nothing will break without you knowing.

Section four dives into optimization. It helps you prepare your entire website for production with a single build step, so you become more efficient and not have to worry about forgetting anything.

Section five talks about the integration phase. It shows you how to integrate your code with a central server. In doing so, we also run tests so you know your team’s code is always up to standard and you don’t have to (once again) worry about things breaking.

Section six shows you different ways to deploy your website quickly onto your servers without having to manually FTP in, copy and paste etc. Updating your site then becomes much easier and less of a headache.

In section seven, we figure out how you can duplicate your workflow for other projects so you don’t have to repeat the steps all over again.

Sounds good?

Well, after you’ve read through the contents, you may be asking if this book is for you.

This book is for you if:

- You want to learn to use better processes to speed up your workflow
- Your want to work less, get more done and become more effective
- You want to have minions doing repetitive tasks so you don’t have to do them anymore (Banana!)
- Finally and most importantly, you want to have more time to spend on stuff that matters.

Nope, you don’t have to be an expert with 5 years of experience before picking this book up. You don’t need to have awesome development knowledge.

**This book is written specifically for people want to have a better workflow, but are not sure how to start and where to start.**

So if you have an interest in improving your workflows, then this book is written for you.
