## **When building components in Astro, you might not want to use Astro files**

When I started building with Astro, I thought most of my components should be built with Astro files.

But after a couple of months building with Astro, my thinking has changed. I no longer build most of my components with astro files. I mostly use Svelte files instead.

Today I want to share with you why I do this — and perhaps you’ll find it useful as you explore Astro and the possibilities it gives you.
