title: How to remember JavaScript
---

JavaScript contains many things — lots of different methods, lots of ways to do things — how do you remember them all?

If you ask around the web, many people will say you don't have to remember everything since you can always google. But this answer doesn't help — you can't always Google everything every time, can you? If you really forget everything and Google everything all the time, you're going to be real slow when you code!

So what's the alternative? What must you remember? What can you forget? That's what today's article is for.
