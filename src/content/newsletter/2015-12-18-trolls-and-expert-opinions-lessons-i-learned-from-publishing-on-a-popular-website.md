---
title: Trolls and Expert Opinions – Lessons I learned from publishing on a popular website
---

As you may know, I recently published an article on Smashing Magazine. It weeks of effort, and I believed that it answered a big question that many people were asking about. I was incredibly proud of it and went to bed feeling awesome.

Unfortunately, many developers (even experts) out there didn’t think it was. They said things like “the author is using Flexbox in the wrong way”, and it was “bizarre” that an article like mine got published on a well-curated website like Smashing Magazine.

How would you feel if you were me?

Well, I tried not to think about it at first. I laughed it off an pretended that nothing bad has happened. It was just a difference in opinion.

In reality though, I felt hurt. Very badly.

Now, after calming down, I thought about the lessons I learned from the whole fiasco, and I wanted to share them with you.

I wanted to share them because I realized the internet isn’t a safe place, and the hard lessons I learned from this incident could potentially help you out if you ever got hurt by the clueless bastards out there.

If you’re interested in how I felt, what I did, and what lessons I got out of this incident, I invite you to read this article:
