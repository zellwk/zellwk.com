By a stroke of luck, my article on how I view [Tailwind’s `@apply`](https://css-tricks.com/tailwinds-apply-feature-is-better-than-it-sounds/) recently dropped on CSS-Tricks.

The gist of the article is:

- Tailwind’s `@apply` is better than most people give it credit for
- It works a lot like Sass mixins, but much more convenient
- We can use it to create utilities (or mixins, if you like that word) that can do lots of work for you.
