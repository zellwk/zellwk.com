How’s life been treating you nowadays? The Coronavirus is getting serious as we speak. More and more people are losing their jobs. Freelancers are losing clients. Some people haven’t lost their jobs yet, but are getting scared shitless.

To make things worse, talented developers (and talented developers to-be) are finding it hard to get a job, ESPECIALLY because this mayhem is going around. Freelancers are finding it hard too.

I’m not trying scare the shit out of you. But this is serious.

**What will YOU do to ensure you survive (or even thrive) during this pandemic?**

If the answer is nothing, you’re not alone. Many people don’t know what they can do. And it might be true that there’s nothing you can do right now…

But we can think forward.

**What will YOU do, so any negative situations that are happening to you now will not affect you the next time a recession or pandemic hits?** (I seriously hope nothing bad is happening to you right now).

Think DEEPLY about this. Lives are at stake here.

What if shit happens and you lose your job? How are you going to support yourself? How are you going to support your spouse and children (if you have any) that are depending on you?

…

I know it sounds harsh. But I want you to think HARD about it. Because we’re talking about your livelihood and the livelihood of people you care about here. It’s no joking matter.

I’m crafting up something SUPER special as we speak. I want to do this for you. **I want to help you recession-proof yourself. So you NEVER fall into a helpless situation EVEN IF a recession hits you in future.**

I’m talking about building yourself a brand.

- So other people know you
- So other people want to hear from you
- So other people want to hire you
- So other people want to help you
- So other people want to learn from you

Once you have great brand around yourself, things become easier.

Imagine if you do these:

1. Send a tweet and ask for a job, and people actually retweet or even give you offers.
2. Tell your friends about your situation and they’ll talk to others to give you a job.
3. Tell the general public that you’re open for employment and people actually take the chance to snap you up. They may actively engage you and you go into discussions and negotiations the very next day.

Wouldn’t that be wonderful?

**The key idea is to learn to build your brand.**
