For the longest time, I thought that “Polymorphing” was about converting something into sheep (thanks to Warcraft). The sheep image stuck with me and made it hard to understand exactly what Polymorphism is.

Today I want to explore what Polymorphism actually is. (Fun fact: Most articles about Polymorphism in JavaScript only 1/3 of what it actually is).

[Click here to read the article!](https://zellwk.com/blog/polymorphism-javascript)

**What you’ll learn**:

There are three kinds of Polymorphism:

1. Adhoc Polymorphism
2. Parametric Polymorphism
3. Subtype Polymorphism (this one is what most people think of as polymorphism)

Fun Fact: The Factory Pattern actually uses Adhoc Polymorphism!
