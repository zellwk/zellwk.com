title: How to choose and pair typefaces

Do you need to choose typefaces for your design? It’s really hard, isn’t it? What do you look out for? How do you pick the right secondary typeface to go along with your chosen one?

Many people get stuck here (especially when designing for their personal projects). I’m not spared from getting stuck either.

Recently, I discovered a simple method to pair typefaces effectively, and I’d love to share them with you. (Hint: it’s a 9×9 grid). I bet you’ll love the article if you found difficulties choosing typefaces :)
