title: Feeling depressed and needing some room
---

I've been feeling a loss about what to write on this blog for the past couple of months, but I continued to push through and produce articles because I thought that was what I should be doing.

When I sat down to write an article for today, emotions overwhelmed me and I can't help but write about what I'm feeling sick and tired about, and what's not working for me in the recent months, or even the whole of 2021 so far.

**Finally, after a couple of hours writing and contemplating, I realized I'm depressed** when it come to work (this blog and the business I've created). **The shocking fact is I've probably been depressed for about 2-3 years without realising it.**

I'm still not done with the article — and it's 1:30 am on my side now. Part of me wants to finish the article and deliver it like how I would do on a normal week. But another part of me wants to give myself time and allow myself to feel the emotions, ponder about what happened, and properly consider what's next before I public say anything.

So **I'm going to stop writing articles for some time. I don't know how this would last**. Maybe it'll end next week, maybe in a couple of weeks, or maybe even in a couple of months.

I just wanted to let you know about this before I go into radio-silence mode regarding the blog.

For UAJS Buyers: I'll still be committed to working on Understanding Asynchronous JavaScript during this period of time, so don't worry. I'll get it done. I'll also poke Hui Jing about Essential CSS.

That's it from me. Hope you are doing fine.
