title: Creating a deep-assign library
---

I created a [library](https://github.com/zellwk/javascript/blob/master/mix) to merge objects last week. It's called `mix`. `mix` lets you perform a deep merge between two objects.

The difference between `mix` and other deep merging libraries is: `mix` lets you copy accessors while others don't.

I thought it'll be fun to share the process (and pains) while building the library. So here it is.

[Here's the article again](https://zellwk.com/blog/creating-a-deep-assign-library). Enjoy!
