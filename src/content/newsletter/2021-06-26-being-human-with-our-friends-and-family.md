## No! Our lives are more than tech and work

I wasn't at the session, but I was curious and engaged enough to find out more (because I really care about you guys after all). After hearing what happened in the session, I came to this thought: **We're all human.**

We all have things we want to talk about. **We have our interests** that sometimes we're afraid to share with other people. It is possible that we've become so engrained into how our industry works, that we forget how to be human with people around us.

**We don't know how to talk in a non-tech way anymore**, and that alienates our friends and family (especially if they don't understand programming).

My wife doesn't understand programming and it's hard for me to talk about anything related to websites, design, creating content — she doesn't care — but that's a large part of what I do every day.

It pains me, for a long time, that all we talk about every day was a variation of the following:

- Hello, how was you day
- Wife complains about work or boss or client
- I listen
- But I have nothing to talk about — I didn't want to complain about my work since I don't want to talk about work during off-office hours.
- She continues to complain or talk about her workplace

She still does it especially because there's so much work stress piling up on her plate. But it's getting better over the years as I start to open up and talk about various topics I'm interested about.

I started sharing deeper thoughts and what I believed with my wife and she came to understand me better. She also began sharing thoughts that she previously kept hidden from me and I got to know here better.

Recently, because we're all so cooped up, I started throwing crazy ideas about touring Singapore in a car (like a mini road-trip) 😂.

So my relationship with my wife is still a WIP, and it has gotten better over the years. The lessons from the Oi Workshop were immensely helpful in allowing me to open up and getting to this point.

**What if you can find topics you're want to talk about with your family and friends** (especially if they're not interested in tech).

How would these conversations change your life?

**Would you light up instead of feeling bored all the time**?

I think it'll be worth exploring
