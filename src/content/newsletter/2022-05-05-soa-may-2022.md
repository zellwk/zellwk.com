## Learn JavaScript

Three things here:

1. Refactored arrow functions to normal functions
2. Fixed some broken links
3. Added content for form elements

**Refactored arrow functions to normal functions**

I made a huge update here by rewriting almost all arrow functions back to normal functions.

Here’s why.

In the past, I thought arrow functions were better than normal functions and I became an arrow-function fanboy. 3 years later, I realized it’s not true. Normal functions were much easier to read in many cases. And hoisting is awesome.

I’m going to rework the hoisting lesson in the follow month and also add a lesson on how I write functions. This should be useful to you.

**Fixing broken links**

I finally fixed the broken links for the Off canvas and Modal accessibility components. If you were looking for them, they’re there now!

Sorry this took so long. I have no excuse.

**Adding content for form elements**

A few days ago I noticed we can loop through `form.elements` to get a unique list of all form fields (input, textarea, etc). I added this into the lesson :)

I had a shift of perspective too — now I’m happy to add new things to LJS when I can find a chance to, without trying to be perfect about each thing I add. Hopefully this benefits all of you who bought the course :)
