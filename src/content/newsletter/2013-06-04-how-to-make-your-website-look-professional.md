Today, I wanted to talk more about how to make your website look like its done by a pro.

One of the main things that many professional designers look out for is the readibility of text used in the website. Along the same notion, they look at the amount of space provided between each line of text and the space provided between each paragraph.

This boils down to the concept of vertical rhythm and I explained it my next blog post that I'll like to share with you. Here's a link if you're ready to jump right in
