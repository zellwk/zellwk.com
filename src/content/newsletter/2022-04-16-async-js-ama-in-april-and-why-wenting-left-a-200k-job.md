## Conversation with Wenting: Leaving a $200k job to create a startup

I had a [great conversation](https://youtu.be/XvwWCujCf9w) with Wenting a couple of weeks back. Wenting used to work at Adobe and she left her $200k/year job to create a startup, [Typogram](https://www.typogram.co/). She’s one of the people behind [CodingFont.com](https://www.codingfont.com/) (a tool that helps you compare different monospace fonts).
