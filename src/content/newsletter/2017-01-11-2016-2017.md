Happy 2017!

How was your 2016? My 2016 was a strange year full of ups and downs. On one hand, I had eye-opening experiences that taught me a lot about myself. On the other hand, I was horrified at the amount of time I wasted accomplishing nothing, so much that I ended the year loathing myself to the core.

But that’s enough. The new year is here. It’s time for me (and you too!) to recollect my experiences and regrets and move on. Here’s an article that summarizes of my learnings in 2016 and my plans for 2017.

[Click here](https://zellwk.com/gotcha#roadmap) here for more information if you are interested in something like this.
