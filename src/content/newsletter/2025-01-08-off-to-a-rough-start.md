It’s Wednesday again and I’m on the hook to deliver the next issue of this newsletter. I’ve failed to deliver it consistently for a while, but I’m changing that this year.

Unfortunately, the year is off to a rough start for me. Late last year, I had to call the suicide hotline for my wife. It’s something I’ve been ashamed to talk about. But I’ve decided that it’s important enough to share.

[https://zellwk.com/articles/suicide-hotline/](https://zellwk.com/articles/suicide-hotline/)

This is important to share because this sort of experience is closer to heart to what I’m creating in next version of Magical Dev School.

I’m not saying we should try to lift people out of undesirable situations. What I’m saying is we all have to go through lousy situations ourselves — and we can support each other as we do.
