title: How I hired an executive assistant that I'm happy with
---

After many failed attempts (and therefore lots of paralysis) with hiring an assistant to help me with my workload, I'm happy to say that I found someone I'm happy with!

Her name is Jen.

Today I want to share how I went through this hiring process — mostly to consolidate my learnings. It'll be a bonus if this helps you work through any kinks when you're at that stage.

[Click here to read the article](https://zellwk.com/blog/hiring-executive-assistant/). Enjoy!

Key ideas:

- Discard your fantasies
- Be honest with yourself to set good expectations
- Filter heavily
- The right person is someone who aligns to the position you're seeking
- Don't hire for a position you're not ready for
- Don't hope that a new hire would grow quickly to what you'll need them to be. It takes a few months of training.

That's it! Hope you find this useful.
