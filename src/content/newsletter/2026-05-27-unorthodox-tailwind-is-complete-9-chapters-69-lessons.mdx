## Updates on Content Systems

[Content Systems](https://zellwk.com/courses/practical-astro/content-systems/) is the first module I'm releasing for [Practical Astro](https://zellwk.com/courses/practical-astro/).

Here, you will find **production-ready patterns I use for all my Astro sites**.

These aren't beginner patterns that you can copy-paste from most tutorials. They're in-depth, useful, and fix almost all of the content problems you may face.

Current patterns include:

1. Using `md` and `mdx` in advanced ways
2. Making content collections easy
3. Quality of life improvements for blogs and tags — one function each
4. Previous, next navigation
5. Pagination
6. Partials (not the Astro kind, but the templating kind)
7. Numbered files (so files are arranged in the order you want)
