Here’s one thing I realized.

I’ve been working alone (at home) for a while now. I don’t talk to people usually because I’m always at home. I rarely go to meetups too.

But I want to talk to people. I want to get to know more people and make more friends.

**So here’s a strange experiment.**

Why don’t we have a chat? Let’s say we met at a “meetup” and we see each other for the first time.

Here are a few rules (made for me):

1. We’ll chat through emails.
2. We can talk about anything
3. I’ll reply to at least one person a day.
4. The conversation must not die on my watch.

(Note: I might take a while to reply to you since there are 19,000 people… but I’ll get there).

Interested?

Let me begin.

Hello! I’m Zell. I’m a developer from Singapore. How’s it going for you?
