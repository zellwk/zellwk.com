If you’re not used to using a linter yet, you will see red squiggly underlines all over your code. it’s hard not to feel like you’re doing something wrong!

But you don’t have to feel that way. A linter is there to help you write better code. There are reasons for the red squiggly underlines. Once you know what brings these underlines out, you’ll know what to ignore and what not to. That’s what you’re going to learn in today’s video.
