Sometimes you may need to put elements with uneven heights or widths into a gallery. When this happens, you know you can’t simply just use the gallery() mixin from Susy.

That’s when a JavaScript solution like Masonry comes in.

What Masonry does specifically is that it helps you to create a pinterest-like gallery easily.

Masonry doesn’t use Susy normally, but that doesn’t mean we can’t marry the two together to make an awesome grid!

You’ll just have to know how these two work and how to pair them up.

In this article, I’ll show you exactly how to combine these two sweet tools.
