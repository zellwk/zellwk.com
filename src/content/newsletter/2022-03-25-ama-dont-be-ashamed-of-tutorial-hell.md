## Don’t be ashamed of tutorial hell

Many people are trapped in tutorial hell — they hop from one tutorial to another, to another, to another, never building something on their own. And they’re ashamed of it.

You don't have to be ashamed if you're going through tutorial hell because it's a natural process of learning.

There's way more depth to what I just said, and I shared more in [this article](https://zellwk.com/blog/dont-be-ashamed-of-tutorial-hell).
