## Breaking the Deadlock

In everything we do or don’t do, there is a deadlock.

This deadlock keeps the balance in our lives so things always remain the same in this deadlock.

So every day you’re stuck doing the same things, asking the same questions, facing the same problems, and feeling accomplished or unaccomplished the same way.

- If you’re stuck in a job, you remain stuck in a job.
- If you’re stuck finding a job, you remain stuck finding a job… until you break out of the deadlock.

Now this deadlock exists because the forces within you have created a “balance” that’s impossible to break out of — unless you know what to do.

Energy is required to break this deadlock so things begin to flow.

To break the deadlock, you have to invest energy into one direction of your life — any direction.

Once you break past a certain barrier, the deadlock unravels and you’re no longer stuck — you can move on to the next thing.

This is why participation allows you to break out of any deadlock — because when you participate put energy into the interaction. And this energy eventually builds up to a point where it unravels the deadlock.

Now here’s one thing you need to know.

This deadlock (or balance) has a name that you know very well.

## It’s called the comfort zone

Most people think that the comfort zone is a place that doesn’t change in size or shape, so they can reach out temporarily, get their goals, and move back into their comfort zone — therefore not having to change anything about themselves.

To picture this…

- You can imagine drawing a circle around you. This circle represents the comfort zone.
- Then, put an apple outside. This apple represents the goal
- Then imagine yourself moving out of the circle, grabbing the apple, and coming back into the circle.

But this is not what actually happens.

**Here’s what the truth looks like.**

- The comfort zone is not a circle
- It’s far better to **imagine the comfort zone as a rubber ball and you’re inside that rubber ball**.
- Everything you want is outside the rubber ball.
- All the fears your want to get rid of are also outside the rubber ball

**When you participate, you stretch this rubber ball** and it expands with the degree of your participation.

**This means you grow your comfort zone when you participate**…

- This is why things that were challenging to you in the past are no longer challenging to you today.
- This is also why things that are challenging to you now will no longer be challenging to you tomorrow if you continue to participate and stretch your comfort zone.

So unless you are happy where you are, you only have one option — and that’s to participate and stretch your comfort zone towards the fulfillment of your goals.

This also means, if you don’t participate in anything beyond your comfort level, you will always remain trapped in the deadlock — living the same day over and over with tiny variations between days.

Now this may be hard to accept so be gentle with yourself as you take it in…

## Participating when it’s hard…

Doing anything beyond what you’re comfortable with is sure to be hard — that’s why you aren’t comfortable with them in the first place.

But as you know now, **this uncomfortable feeling will soon pass, because you will grow and create a new comfort zone**.

So in the meantime…

- If you’re afraid, summon your courage
- If you’re unwilling, summon your willingness.
- If you’re hesitant, summon your decisiveness.

They’re all somewhere within you. And they will respond when you call for them. You don’t have to “fake it” to make it.

The hardest part is breaking out of your comfort zone the first time.

When you can do it once, you can do it many times — and you have already broken out of your comfort zone at least once in your life, what’s there to fear?

**You are stronger, more capable, more powerful, more persuasive, more intelligent, wiser, and more willing than who you think you are.**

You just haven’t been able to accept some of the qualities that you have shown momentarily in the past yet.

So now is the time for you to break out of your deadlock and accept those qualities for good.

Once you start, you can use the momentum to build up the tiny snowball into an unstoppable avalanche.

When you do this, nothing will stop you from making your dreams a reality…

But you don’t have to believe what I say.

Just try it out for yourself — put in some energy and overcome the inertia — and you get a glimpse of how powerful you actually are.
