It’s the end of 2017; take a moment and think about how the year has passed for you. Were you able to overcome the challenges you faced, or did you get stuck and give up midway?

When I was 26 (that’s three years ago, in 2014), I was deathly afraid of Javascript. I couldn’t build anything I want from scratch, and I was sick of googling and copy-pasting other people’s code. I already tried to learn Javascript, but I’ve always failed at it, even though I’ve read books and went through courses.

In 2015, I resolved myself to learn Javascript properly. This resolve changed everything.

Somehow, I managed a breakthrough in two months. I learned so much about Javascript that I was no longer afraid of it; I can even build plugins for components that can’t be found elsewhere on the internet.

It’s strange how life works sometimes. When you resolve yourself (like really resolve, and firmly believe in your resolve), things happen, and you get a chance to break through your barriers.

How did I break through? Well, I got into a weird encounter with a client that forced me to learn Javascript. I’ll share more about the encounter with my client and how I broke through my barriers to learning Javascript tomorrow.
