---
title: Why you shouldn't try to use color palette galleries and colour picker tools
---

I've been thinking recently alot about how I tried to learn about design and the problem with colors really stood out to me.

You see, when I started out, I often jumped at color palettes and color pickers to find colors for my designs. Unfortunately, I was always unable to find something that work out in the end.

Have you tried using them?

If not, or even if you did, I suggest you to stop and try picking your own colors by observing how other people choose colors for their websites instead.

Here's an article to why I wouldn't recommend using them.
